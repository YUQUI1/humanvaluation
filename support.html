Human Valuation PRO

Human Valuation PRO offers monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscription options.

Purchases and subscription billing are processed securely by Apple through the App Store.

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased Human Valuation PRO and your subscription is not recognized, open the subscription screen inside the app and select Restore Purchases.

Manage or Cancel a Subscription

Subscriptions can be managed or canceled through your Apple ID subscription settings.

Canceling a subscription stops future automatic renewals. Access may continue until the end of the current paid subscription period.

About Human Valuation

The dollar-style Human Valuation shown in the app is a fictional model index for personal reflection and self-development.

It is not net worth, income, salary, credit score, investment value, or a prediction of future earnings.
