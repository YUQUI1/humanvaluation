Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Human Valuation is designed to help users reflect on personal growth, strengths, and personal capital while minimizing the collection of personal information.

1. Information You Enter

Human Valuation may ask you to provide information about your skills, experience, finances, goals, habits, and other personal-capital factors in order to generate your valuation report.

These assessment answers and saved valuation records are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share content.

2. Accounts

Human Valuation does not require users to create an account or sign in.

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

Human Valuation PRO subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

Human Valuation does not receive or store your full payment card information.

4. Photos and Sharing

If you choose to save a share card to your Photos library, the app may request permission to add the generated image to your library.

5. Tracking and Advertising

Human Valuation does not use third-party advertising and does not track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

6. AI and External Processing

The core Human Valuation calculation and summary features are generated using local app logic and model rules.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored Human Valuation data remains on your device until you delete the data, remove the app, or otherwise clear the app's local data.

8. Children

Human Valuation is not designed specifically for children and is not presented as a children's app.

9. Disclaimer

The dollar-style Human Valuation displayed in the app is a fictional model index for personal reflection and self-development.

It is not your net worth, income, salary, credit score, investment value, or a prediction of future earnings.

Human Valuation does not provide financial, investment, employment, medical, or legal advice.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
